# Render assets

Textures (`pbr/`), the studio HDRI (`brown_photostudio_02_1k.hdr`) and the prop
models (`models/`) are from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), licensed CC0
(public domain). They were downloaded on 2026-09-18 for the furnishes-house
prototype and copied here unchanged.

They are used only to render the images in `public/renders/studio` and
`public/renders/scene`. The props (chair, plant, basket) are scene dressing and
are not Furnishes products.
